Not a medical service

The CADASIL Global Foundation provides educational and community support. We do not provide medical advice, diagnose, or treat. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency number. For clinical guidance, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

A note on response times

We are a small team — but we will respond.

Most messages are answered within a few business days. Patients and families with urgent questions are prioritized. If a message is time-sensitive, please mention it.
